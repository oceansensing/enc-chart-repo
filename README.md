# enc-chart-repo

The land of NOAA's nautical charts, as vector tiles for the oceansensing.org
ocean map: the Electronic Navigational Charts' land areas, each place from the
finest usage band that charts it, with the charts' coverage, and inside US
waters, where no chart covers a place within 2 km of Natural Earth's land,
that place as land. It is the land an ocean field is clipped at along US
coasts.

Updated monthly.

## Sources and credits

NOAA Office of Coast Survey, ENC Direct to GIS: every usage band's land and coverage layers, a U.S. government work in the public domain.
